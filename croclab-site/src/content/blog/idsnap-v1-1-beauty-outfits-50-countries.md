When we launched [IDSnap](/idsnap/) three weeks ago, the idea was simple: take a photo, remove the background, crop to spec, done. No servers, no subscriptions, no privacy concerns. A passport photo maker that does one thing and does it well.

Then the feedback started rolling in.

*"Can you add a skin smoothing option? My photo looks too harsh under fluorescent light."*

*"I'm applying for a Brazilian visa — do you have Brazil's spec?"*

We listened. IDSnap v1.1 ships with every feature users asked for — and a few they didn't know they wanted.

## What's New in v1.1

### Light Beauty Mode

Not everyone has perfect studio lighting at home. Fluorescent bulbs create harsh shadows. Phone cameras pick up every pore and blemish. For an ID photo you'll use on official documents for years, a little polish goes a long way.

IDSnap v1.1 adds a beauty toggle with two adjustable controls:

- **Skin smoothing** — Powered by CoreImage filters running entirely on your device. Smooths out minor blemishes, reduces pore visibility, and evens skin tone. Adjustable from subtle to noticeable.
- **Brightness adjustment** — Brighten a slightly underexposed photo without washing out colors.

The key word is *light*. This isn't Instagram-filter territory. The goal is a clean, professional look that still looks like you — because that's what government reviewers expect.

> **Everything stays on your device.** Beauty processing uses Apple's CoreImage framework locally. No photos are uploaded anywhere. Same privacy-first approach as every other IDSnap feature.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the beauty mode too heavy? Will my photo look fake?**
A: No. IDSnap's beauty mode is intentionally subtle — it smooths minor blemishes and evens skin tone without altering your appearance. Government ID photos need to look like you. The adjustable slider lets you find the right balance.

**Q: Do the outfit templates look realistic?**
A: The templates are designed to blend naturally with common photo backgrounds (white, blue, gray). They work best with front-facing photos taken from chest level up. The overlay aligns with your shoulders automatically.

**Q: Are all 50+ country specs accurate?**
A: Yes. Each specification follows the official government requirements for that country's passport, visa, or national ID. We reference the published standards from each country's immigration or identity authority.

**Q: Does the widget require any setup?**
A: No. After updating to v1.1, the IDSnap widget appears in your widget gallery. Add it to your home screen and it works immediately — one tap to launch the camera.

**Q: Is v1.1 still free?**
A: Yes, completely free. All new features — beauty mode, outfit templates, expanded country specs, multi-language, widget — are included at no cost. The app is supported by non-intrusive ads.
