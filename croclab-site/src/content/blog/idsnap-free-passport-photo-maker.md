## How IDSnap Works

### 1. Take or Select a Photo

Open IDSnap and either take a new photo using your iPhone camera or pick an existing one from your photo library. No special background or lighting required — IDSnap handles imperfect conditions.

### 2. AI Face Detection

Apple's Vision framework detects your face position, eye alignment, and head boundaries. IDSnap uses this data to ensure your photo meets official requirements for face centering and proportion.

### 3. Instant Background Swap

Using on-device person segmentation, IDSnap removes your original background and replaces it with the standard color required for your document type:

- **White** — US passport, Schengen visa, most international documents
- **Blue** — US visa, some Southeast Asian IDs
- **Red** — China mainland ID photos
- **Custom colors** — For any other requirement

The segmentation runs entirely on your iPhone's Neural Engine — your photo never touches a server.

## FAQ

**Q: Is IDSnap really free?**
A: Yes, completely free. All features — face detection, background removal, all 12+ country specs, print layout — are available at no cost. The app is supported by non-intrusive ads.

**Q: Does IDSnap upload my photos to any server?**
A: No. All processing happens entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. Your photos never leave your device through our app. See our [privacy policy](/idsnap/privacy/) for details.

**Q: Can I use IDSnap photos for official documents?**
A: IDSnap follows official specifications for each country and document type. However, acceptance ultimately depends on the reviewing authority. We recommend checking your specific requirements and using a well-lit, front-facing photo for best results.

**Q: What iPhone models are supported?**
A: IDSnap requires iOS 16.0 or later. It works on iPhone 8 and newer models. Face detection and background removal work best on devices with the Neural Engine (iPhone XS and later).

**Q: Can I print IDSnap photos at home?**
A: Yes. IDSnap generates a 4×6 inch print layout with multiple photo copies and crop lines. Load standard 4×6 photo paper into any home printer, print, and cut along the lines.
